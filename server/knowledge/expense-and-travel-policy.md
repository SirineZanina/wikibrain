# Expense and Travel Policy

**Aster Dynamics, Inc.**
Policy ID: FIN-003 · Version 6.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Finance
Supersedes: FIN-003 v6.0 (effective 1 January 2025)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs money spent by employees on behalf of Aster Dynamics: what may be spent, who
approves it, how it is claimed, and when it is reimbursed.

It applies to all employees, contractors, and any person travelling at Company expense. It covers
business travel, client entertainment, subscriptions, training, and any out-of-pocket purchase
made for Company purposes.

The governing principle is that an expense must be **necessary, reasonable, and actually incurred**
in the conduct of Company business. Where this policy does not address a specific situation, the
test is whether the employee would be comfortable having the expense published to the whole
company. If not, it should not be claimed.

Personal expenses of any kind must not be charged to a Company card, even with the intention of
repaying. This is the single most common cause of card suspension.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

Approval is required **before** the expense is committed, not after it appears on a claim.

| Amount (USD or local equivalent) | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $150 | No pre-approval; manager approves at claim |
| $151 – $2,500 | Direct manager, in advance |
| $2,501 – $10,000 | Director, in advance |
| $10,001 – $50,000 | VP of the function plus Finance Business Partner |
| Over $50,000 | CFO, and where recurring, the Executive Committee |

Splitting a single purchase into smaller claims to stay under a threshold is a disciplinary matter.

Any expense committing the Company to a recurring cost — a subscription, a licence, a retainer —
requires Finance approval regardless of monthly amount, because the relevant figure is the annual
committed value.

## 3. Travel Booking

### 3.1 Booking channel

All flights, rail, and hotels must be booked through the Company travel platform (TravelHub) or the
Company travel desk. Bookings made outside the platform are reimbursed only where the platform was
unavailable and the employee has documented this at the time.

Travel must be booked at least **14 days in advance** wherever practical. Bookings made inside 7
days of departure require director approval, because short-notice fares are typically two to three
times the advance fare.

### 3.2 Air travel

| Scheduled flight duration | Permitted class |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| 10 hours or more, arriving with under 12 hours before a working commitment | Business |

Business class otherwise requires VP approval. Flight duration is measured per leg, excluding
layovers.

Employees may upgrade at their own cost, and may retain personal frequent-flyer benefits accrued on
Company travel. Employees must not select a more expensive itinerary in order to accrue status; the
travel platform records the lowest logical fare at time of booking and material deviations are
reported to Finance monthly.

Where an employee has a documented medical or accessibility need, seating and class
accommodations are arranged through People Operations and are not charged against the team's travel
budget or subject to the table above.

### 3.3 Rail

Rail travel is preferred over air on any route where scheduled city-centre-to-city-centre journey
time is under 5 hours. Standard class for journeys under 3 hours; first class permitted for
journeys of 3 hours or more where it is cheaper than the equivalent flight plus transfers.

### 3.4 Accommodation

Hotels are booked through TravelHub within the nightly caps below, excluding tax:

| Location tier | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 — New York, San Francisco, London, Singapore, Tokyo, Zurich | **$320** |
| Tier 2 — other major metropolitan areas | **$240** |
| Tier 3 — all other locations | **$170** |

Where no compliant room is available within the cap, the platform will surface an over-cap option;
selecting it requires a one-line justification and director approval. Employees must not book a
non-compliant hotel outside the platform to avoid this step.

Aster Dynamics does not reimburse room upgrades, in-room minibar, in-room movies, spa, or laundry
for trips under 5 nights. Laundry is reimbursable for trips of **5 nights or more**.

Employees may stay with friends or family instead of a hotel and claim a flat **$50 per night**
with no receipt, capped at the applicable nightly cap for that location.

### 3.5 Travel safety and duty of care

Every business trip must be recorded in TravelHub before departure, including trips booked outside
the platform under the exception in Section 3.1. This is a safety obligation, not an
administrative one: in an incident, the Company must be able to identify within minutes who is
where.

Travel to any country rated **High Risk or above** on the Company travel risk register requires
approval from the Security team and completion of the pre-travel briefing, regardless of trip
length or cost. Travel to Extreme Risk locations requires VP approval and is arranged with the
Company's travel risk provider.

All travelling employees are covered by the Company travel insurance policy, which includes
medical evacuation. The 24-hour assistance number is printed on the digital travel card issued
with every booking. Employees should contact that number before incurring emergency medical costs
wherever it is safe to do so; costs incurred without contact are still covered, but reimbursement
takes longer.

Employees may decline travel they believe to be unsafe, and are not required to give a detailed
reason. Declining travel on safety grounds carries no performance consequence.

### 3.6 Rest and trip length

Employees travelling on a flight of **8 hours or more** are entitled to a rest day on arrival or
on return, taken as a working day rather than as leave. Employees should not be scheduled into
customer meetings within **6 hours** of a long-haul arrival.

Weekend travel is compensated with a day in lieu where the travel occupies more than four hours of
a non-working day.

## 4. Meals and Per Diem

### 4.1 Per diem while travelling

Aster Dynamics operates a per diem for meals on travel days. No receipts are required, and the per
diem is paid regardless of actual spend.

| Location tier | Daily per diem |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | **$85** |
| Tier 2 | **$65** |
| Tier 3 | **$45** |

Per diem is reduced by **$20 for each meal provided** by a conference, hotel rate, customer, or
Company event. On a day where all meals are provided, no per diem is payable.

Partial travel days are paid at 50% of the daily rate where the employee departs after 14:00 or
returns before 12:00.

Where an employee is required by a customer contract to submit actual receipts rather than per
diem, the per diem is not paid and actual reasonable meal costs are reimbursed instead, capped at
the per diem rate plus 50%.

### 4.2 Alcohol

Alcohol is not claimable on per diem. Alcohol at a client entertainment event (Section 5) is
claimable within the limits of that section. Aster Dynamics does not reimburse alcohol consumed
alone or charged to a hotel room.

### 4.3 Team meals

A manager may host a team meal at up to **$60 per attendee** including tax and service, up to four
times per year per team. Meals above that value or frequency require director approval. The
attendee list must be recorded in the claim.

## 5. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment must have a clear business purpose and a named attendee list. The limit is
**$150 per attendee** including Company attendees, tax, and service.

Every entertainment claim must record: the names and employers of all attendees, the business
purpose, and the date. Claims without an attendee list are rejected automatically.

Entertainment involving government officials, public-sector employees, or state-owned enterprise
staff is **prohibited without prior Legal approval**, at any value including zero. Gifts to any
customer or prospect above **$75** likewise require Legal approval. This is an anti-bribery
control, not a cost control, and there are no exceptions for local custom.

## 6. Ground Transport

Standard, non-premium ride-hailing and taxi fares are reimbursable for business travel. Premium
tiers are not reimbursable except where standard is unavailable or where the employee is
travelling with a client.

Rental cars are permitted at compact or intermediate class where they are cheaper than the
equivalent taxi journeys, or where no practical alternative exists. The Company's insurance covers
rental vehicles used for business in all operating countries; employees should decline the rental
company's collision damage waiver, which is not reimbursable.

Employees using a personal vehicle for business are reimbursed at the prevailing statutory mileage
rate for their location. Commuting between home and an assigned office is never reimbursable,
regardless of work arrangement.

Airport parking is reimbursable up to **$25 per day** for a maximum of 7 days; beyond 7 days,
return taxi fares are expected to be cheaper and are the reimbursable option.

## 7. Other Reimbursable Categories

- **Professional subscriptions and memberships:** up to **$500 per year** with manager approval
  where relevant to the role.
- **Training and conferences:** up to **$2,500 per employee per year** with manager approval.
  Amounts above this are handled under the Learning and Development budget and require director
  approval.
- **Books and learning materials:** up to **$300 per year**, no pre-approval required.
- **Visas, vaccinations, and travel insurance** required for business travel: fully reimbursable.
- **Baggage:** one checked bag per leg. A second bag requires a stated reason.
- **In-flight connectivity:** reimbursable on flights of 3 hours or more.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines, airline change fees arising from
personal preference, personal travel legs added to a business trip, travel for accompanying family
members, personal grooming, childcare and pet care, hotel loyalty scheme fees, credit card interest
and late fees, personal insurance, political donations, and any charitable donation not made under
the Company matching programme.

Expenses incurred for the private benefit of the employee are not reimbursable even where they
arise during business travel.

## 9. Claims and Reimbursement

Claims are submitted in Expensify. An itemised receipt is required for **every expense over $25**,
except per diem and the $50 accommodation allowance in Section 3.4. A card statement line is not a
receipt; the receipt must show what was purchased.

Claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 60
days require director approval and are approved only in exceptional circumstances. Claims older
than **120 days** are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Approved claims are paid in the next scheduled reimbursement run. Runs are processed **twice
monthly**, on the 15th and the last working day of the month. Payment reaches the employee's
account within **5 working days** of the run.

Foreign currency expenses are converted at the rate applied by the Company card provider on the
transaction date, or where paid personally, at the mid-market rate on the transaction date as
sourced by Expensify. Employees should not convert manually.

### 9.1 Cash and unreceipted expenses

Where a receipt is genuinely unobtainable — a metered taxi, a market stall, a tip in a cash
economy — the employee submits a missing-receipt declaration in Expensify stating what was bought,
where, and why no receipt exists. Missing-receipt declarations are limited to **$200 per trip**
and **6 occurrences per year**. Beyond that, claims require director approval.

Cash withdrawn for business use in locations where card payment is impractical must be requested
in advance from Finance as a travel float, and reconciled within **10 working days** of return.
Unspent float is returned; it is not retained against future travel.

### 9.2 Advance payment

Employees who would face hardship funding business travel personally may request payment in
advance rather than reimbursement. Requests go to Finance at least 10 working days before travel
and are granted as a matter of routine. No employee is expected to carry Company travel costs on a
personal credit line.

## 10. Company Cards

Company cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times per year or who hold
purchasing responsibility. Application is through Finance with director approval.

Cardholders must reconcile every transaction in Expensify within **30 days**. Transactions
unreconciled after 45 days trigger an automatic reminder to the cardholder and their manager;
after 60 days the card is suspended until reconciliation is complete.

Cash advances on Company cards are disabled and must not be requested.

## 11. Audit and Consequences

Finance audits a random sample of claims each month, plus all claims over $2,500 and all
entertainment claims. Employees may be asked to provide additional detail up to **24 months** after
a claim.

Knowingly submitting a false or inflated claim is fraud. It is treated as gross misconduct, may
result in dismissal, and where material will be referred for prosecution. Genuine errors are not
misconduct — an employee who identifies and reports their own error will not be penalised.

## 12. Exceptions and Questions

Exceptions require written approval from the CFO. Policy questions go to the Finance service desk.
Booking questions go to the travel desk. Suspected fraud should be reported through the confidential
Speak Up channel, which accepts anonymous reports.
