# PTO and Leave Policy

**Aster Dynamics, Inc.**
Policy ID: HR-007 · Version 5.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Supersedes: HR-007 v4.3 (effective 1 July 2024)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out every category of paid and unpaid time away from work at Aster Dynamics, how
much of each an employee is entitled to, and how it is requested and approved.

It applies to all full-time and part-time employees on Aster Dynamics payroll. Part-time employees
receive all entitlements in this policy on a pro-rata basis calculated on contracted hours, except
bereavement leave (Section 7) and jury service (Section 8), which are not prorated.

Every figure in this policy is a **minimum**. Where a country's statutory entitlement exceeds the
figure stated here, the statutory entitlement applies and is published in the country addendum for
that location. Aster Dynamics never pays below a statutory floor.

All leave is requested in Workday. Leave taken without an approved Workday record is unrecorded
absence and is handled by the employee's manager.

## 2. Annual Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue **25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**, accruing at
**2.0833 days per completed month of service**. Accrual begins on the first day of employment.

Length-of-service increases are applied on the 1 January following the anniversary:

| Completed years of service | Annual entitlement |
|---|---|
| 0 – 2 years | 25 days |
| 3 – 5 years | 27 days |
| 6 – 9 years | 29 days |
| 10 years and over | 32 days |

Public holidays (Section 3) are **in addition** to annual leave and are never deducted from it.

### 2.2 Booking and approval

Annual leave is requested in Workday and approved by the direct manager. Requests should be
submitted with reasonable notice:

- **1 to 2 days:** at least 3 working days' notice.
- **3 to 9 days:** at least 10 working days' notice.
- **10 days or more:** at least 30 calendar days' notice.

Managers must respond to a leave request within **5 working days**. A request that receives no
response within 5 working days is escalated automatically in Workday to the manager's manager.

A manager may decline a request only for a documented business reason — most commonly a release
window, an audit period, or a coverage conflict where too large a share of a team is already
absent. A declined request must be accompanied by a stated reason and, where possible, an
alternative window. Managers may not decline leave on the grounds that a project is generally
busy.

Aster Dynamics does not operate a blackout period across the whole company. Individual teams may
designate up to **10 working days per year** as restricted, published at least one quarter in
advance.

### 2.3 Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar
year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, after which they lapse without payment.

Carryover above 5 days is approved only where the employee was prevented from taking leave by
business need or by long-term sickness, and requires approval from the employee's director. Where
an employee has been on maternity, paternity, adoption, or shared parental leave for any part of
the leave year, full carryover of untaken entitlement is granted automatically and the 31 March
deadline does not apply.

### 2.4 Payment on leaving

On termination of employment, accrued and untaken annual leave is paid in the final pay run at the
employee's daily rate. Where an employee has taken more leave than accrued at the point of leaving,
the excess is deducted from final pay where local law permits.

### 2.5 Minimum usage

Employees are expected to take at least **10 annual leave days per year**, of which at least
**5 must be consecutive**. This is a wellbeing requirement, not an administrative one. Managers
review leave balances at the mid-year checkpoint and are accountable for employees who are
significantly under-using leave.

### 2.6 Part-time and irregular hours

Part-time employees receive annual leave pro-rata to contracted hours. A person contracted for
three days a week receives **15 days** against the 25-day full-time entitlement, and public
holidays are likewise prorated so that no employee gains or loses by the days of the week they
happen to work.

Leave is deducted in the units the employee actually works. An employee working four longer days
per week has one day deducted for one day absent, not a fraction based on hours.

Employees who change between full-time and part-time mid-year have entitlement recalculated from
the date of change. Leave already taken at the previous rate is not recalculated retrospectively.

### 2.7 Buying and selling leave

During the annual benefits window each November, employees may **buy up to 5 additional days** or
**sell up to 3 unused days**, in whole days only. Bought days are deducted from pay in equal
instalments across the following 12 months. Sold days are paid in the January pay run.

Employees cannot sell below the statutory minimum for their location. Bought days do not carry
over and are lost if unused at year end.

## 3. Public Holidays

Employees receive the public holidays of their registered work location. The number varies by
country; the authoritative list for each location is published in Workday each October for the
following year.

In addition to statutory public holidays, Aster Dynamics closes globally for **three Company Days**
per year: 2 January, the Friday of the first full week in July, and 24 December. Company Days are
paid and are not deducted from annual leave.

Where an employee is required to work on a public holiday — for example while on an on-call
rotation — they receive a day in lieu, to be taken within **90 days**, plus the on-call
compensation set out in the Compensation Policy.

### 3.1 Religious and cultural observance

The published public holidays of any location will not match every employee's observance. Any
employee may **swap up to 3 public holidays per year** for days of their own choosing, by
recording the swap in Workday at least 14 days in advance. Swapped days are worked as normal
working days and the substituted days are taken as paid holiday.

Managers approve swap requests as a matter of course. A swap may only be declined for the same
documented coverage reasons that apply to annual leave, and never on the basis of which observance
is involved.

### 3.2 Volunteering leave

Employees receive **2 paid days per calendar year** to volunteer with a registered charity or
community organisation. Volunteering days do not carry over. Team-wide volunteering events
organised by the Company do not count against this allowance.

## 4. Sick Leave

### 4.1 Short-term sickness

Employees receive **12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**. Sick leave does not accrue
monthly; the full entitlement is available from day one of each year and does not carry over.

Sick leave is for the employee's own illness, injury, medical appointments that cannot be scheduled
outside working hours, and mental health. Employees notify their manager as early as practical on
the first day of absence and record it in Workday on return.

A medical certificate is required for any absence of **more than 5 consecutive working days**.
Aster Dynamics does not require a certificate for shorter absences.

### 4.2 Long-term sickness

Absence beyond 12 days in a year, or any single absence beyond 20 consecutive working days, moves
to the long-term sickness process managed by People Operations. Under that process the employee
receives:

- **Weeks 1 – 13:** 100% of base salary.
- **Weeks 14 – 26:** 75% of base salary.
- **Beyond week 26:** statutory sick pay for the location, plus any income protection benefit for
  which the employee is eligible.

Long-term sickness is managed case by case with occupational health input. Annual leave continues
to accrue throughout.

### 4.3 Caring for others

Employees receive **5 days of paid carer's leave per calendar year** to care for a dependant. This
is separate from and additional to sick leave. It is intended for short-term and unforeseen needs —
a child sent home from school, an elderly relative discharged from hospital, the breakdown of a
regular care arrangement.

## 5. Parental Leave

Entitlements below are Company minimums and apply regardless of gender, and equally to birth,
surrogacy, and adoption. Eligibility requires **26 weeks of continuous service** at the expected
date of birth or placement.

### 5.1 Primary caregiver

**18 weeks at 100% of base salary.** The employee designates themselves primary caregiver in
Workday; the Company does not require evidence of which parent is primary.

Primary caregiver leave must start within 4 weeks of the birth or placement and may be taken in up
to **three separate blocks** within the first 18 months, with each block a minimum of two weeks.

### 5.2 Secondary caregiver

**8 weeks at 100% of base salary**, to be taken within the first 12 months, in a minimum of one-week
blocks.

### 5.3 Phased return

On returning from any parental leave of 8 weeks or more, employees may work at **80% of contracted
hours for 100% of pay for the first 4 weeks** back. This is automatic and does not require
approval.

### 5.4 Pregnancy loss

Employees and their partners receive **10 days of paid leave** following a pregnancy loss at any
stage. No evidence is required and no explanation is expected beyond selecting the category in
Workday.

### 5.5 Fertility treatment

Employees undergoing fertility treatment receive **5 days of paid leave per calendar year** for
appointments and recovery, in addition to sick leave.

## 6. Sabbatical

Employees with **5 years of continuous service** are eligible for a **4-week paid sabbatical**,
taken in a single block, in addition to annual leave.

A further 4-week sabbatical is available at 10 years of service, and every 5 years thereafter.
Sabbaticals must be requested at least **90 days in advance** and approved by the employee's
director. Entitlement does not accumulate: an unused sabbatical is not carried forward and is not
paid out on leaving.

Sabbatical time counts as continuous service for all other entitlements.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

| Relationship | Paid leave |
|---|---|
| Partner, child, or parent | **20 days** |
| Sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or parent-in-law | **10 days** |
| Other close relationship, including chosen family | **5 days** |

Bereavement leave is not prorated for part-time employees. It need not be taken consecutively and
may be taken at any point within **12 months** of the death. Aster Dynamics does not require
documentation for bereavement leave.

Where an employee needs more time than the table provides, additional paid leave is granted at the
manager's discretion and is not deducted from annual leave.

## 8. Public Duties

Paid leave is granted, without limit and without deduction from any other entitlement, for jury
service, service as a magistrate, attendance as a witness under subpoena, and service in a
statutory reserve force up to **15 days per year**.

Employees must forward any court or state allowance received to Payroll, which offsets it against
salary paid for that period.

## 9. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to **3 months** may be granted at the discretion of the employee's director,
where the request cannot reasonably be met by any other category in this policy. Requests are
submitted at least 60 days in advance.

During unpaid leave, annual leave does not accrue. Health benefits continue for the first 3 months
at Company cost. Equity vesting is paused for any unpaid leave period exceeding **30 consecutive
days**, and the vesting schedule extends by the length of the pause.

## 10. Leave Interaction Rules

- Annual leave and sick leave cannot be taken on the same day. Where an employee falls sick during
  booked annual leave, the affected days are reclassified as sick leave on request, and the annual
  leave days are returned to the employee's balance.
- Public holidays falling within a period of annual leave are not deducted from annual leave.
- Public holidays falling within parental leave do not extend the parental leave period.
- Carer's leave, bereavement leave, and public duties leave are never deducted from annual leave or
  sick leave.

## 11. Exceptions and Questions

Exceptions require written approval from the VP of People Operations. Questions about entitlement
go to the People Operations service desk. Questions about how leave appears in pay go to Payroll.
