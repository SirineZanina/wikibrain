# Remote Work Policy

**Aster Dynamics, Inc.**
Policy ID: HR-014 · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Supersedes: HR-014 v3.1 (effective 1 March 2025)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how employees of Aster Dynamics may work outside a Company office, what the
Company will pay for, and what obligations remain unchanged regardless of where an employee sits.

It applies to all full-time and part-time employees on Aster Dynamics payroll worldwide.
Contractors, agency staff, and vendor personnel are covered by their engagement agreements and by
the Contractor Security Addendum, not by this policy. Interns are covered, except for the
relocation provisions in Section 9.

Where this policy conflicts with a local statutory requirement, the local requirement prevails and
People Operations will publish a country addendum.

## 2. Work Arrangements

Aster Dynamics operates three formal arrangements. Every employee has exactly one, recorded in
Workday, and it is set at the time of offer.

### 2.1 Office-based

The employee works from an assigned Company office at least four days per week. A permanently
assigned desk is provided. This arrangement is required for roles with a hardware, lab, physical
security, or facilities component.

### 2.2 Hybrid

The employee works from an assigned Company office a minimum of **eight days per calendar month**,
with at least **two of those days falling in the same week as their team's anchor day**. Anchor
days are set per team by the team's director and published one quarter in advance. Hybrid employees
are assigned a hot desk through the Deskbird booking system rather than a permanent desk.

Attendance is measured monthly, not weekly. An employee who is on approved leave for part of a
month has the eight-day minimum prorated by the number of working days actually available to them,
rounded down.

### 2.3 Fully remote

The employee has no office attendance requirement. Fully remote employees are expected to attend
**two in-person team gatherings per year** (see Section 10); travel for those gatherings is booked
under the Expense and Travel Policy and is not charged to the employee's team budget.

Fully remote status requires approval from both the employee's director and the People Operations
Business Partner for the function. Approval is granted against the role, not the person: if the
employee moves into a role classified as office-based, the arrangement changes with the role.

## 3. Eligibility

New employees are eligible for hybrid arrangements from their start date. Fully remote
arrangements require **90 days of continuous service** before they take effect, except where the
role was posted as remote-first, in which case the arrangement applies immediately.

An employee on a formal performance improvement plan is not eligible to change arrangement while
the plan is active. Existing arrangements are not withdrawn solely because a plan has been opened.

## 4. Core Hours and Availability

Aster Dynamics runs asynchronously by default, but three commitments are mandatory:

- **Core hours** are **10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's registered work location time zone.**
  Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours on working days.
- Employees whose team spans more than five time zones must maintain **at least three hours of
  daily overlap** with their team's designated collaboration window.
- Calendars must be kept current. Any recurring unavailability of more than two hours during core
  hours must appear as a calendar block.

Employees may shift their working day by up to two hours in either direction without approval by
updating their working hours in Google Calendar. Shifts larger than two hours, or any permanent
change to the registered time zone, require manager approval and a Workday update.

Response-time expectation is **one working day** for email and internal ticket comments, and **four
working hours** during core hours for direct messages marked urgent. There is no expectation of
response outside an employee's working hours, on weekends, or during approved leave.

### 4.1 Meeting and communication norms

Distributed teams fail through meeting sprawl more often than through under-communication. The
following are mandatory:

- Every recurring meeting must have a written agenda circulated at least **12 hours** in advance.
  A recurring meeting with no agenda is cancelled by default.
- Meetings default to **25 or 50 minutes**, not 30 or 60, so that back-to-back attendees have a
  break.
- Any meeting with more than **8 attendees** requires a named facilitator and a written summary
  posted within one working day.
- **Wednesday is a no-recurring-meeting day** company-wide. Ad-hoc and customer meetings are
  permitted; standing internal meetings are not.
- If every attendee is remote, the meeting is remote for everyone. Employees in an office join
  from their own device rather than as a room, so that no participant is disadvantaged by a shared
  camera and microphone.

Decisions must be recorded in writing in the relevant project space, not left in a meeting or a
direct message. A decision that exists only in a call is not a decision that a distributed team
can act on.

Cameras are optional on all internal calls. Employees must not be asked to justify keeping a
camera off.

### 4.2 Quiet hours

No employee is expected to send or respond to messages between **20:00 and 07:00** in their own
time zone. Where a message must be composed outside those hours, the sender uses scheduled send.
Repeated out-of-hours messaging to the same individual is a management issue and will be raised
with the sender's manager.

## 5. Home Office Stipend and Equipment

### 5.1 Initial setup stipend

Every employee with a hybrid or fully remote arrangement receives a one-time home office setup
stipend:

| Arrangement | Initial stipend |
|---|---|
| Fully remote | **$1,500** |
| Hybrid | **$750** |
| Office-based | Not eligible |

The stipend is claimed through Expensify under the category `Home Office — Setup`, and must be
claimed within **120 days of the arrangement start date**. Unclaimed amounts expire and are not
paid out as cash. Receipts are required for any single item over $75.

Eligible purchases include desks, chairs, monitors, monitor arms, keyboards, mice, desk lamps,
webcams, headsets, footrests, and surge protectors. Not eligible: rent, mortgage interest,
utilities other than internet (see 5.3), furniture for rooms not used for work, artwork, plants,
coffee equipment, or any item primarily for personal use.

### 5.2 Refresh stipend

After the initial stipend, employees may claim a **$400 refresh stipend once every 24 months**,
measured from the date of the last approved home office claim. The refresh stipend follows the same
eligibility rules and the same category in Expensify.

### 5.3 Internet and mobile

Fully remote employees receive a flat **$65 per month** internet allowance, paid through payroll
and taxed as income where local law requires. Hybrid employees receive **$35 per month**.
Office-based employees are not eligible. No receipts are required; the allowance is flat and is not
adjusted for actual cost.

Employees whose role requires on-call participation additionally receive a **$40 per month** mobile
allowance for the months in which they are rostered on-call.

### 5.4 Company equipment

The Company issues, and retains ownership of, one laptop, one external display, and one headset per
employee. Standard issue is refreshed on a **36-month cycle**. Employees may request an early
refresh through IT if hardware fails or if role requirements change; early refresh requires
manager approval.

Company equipment is for Company work. It must not be used by family members or shared. Equipment
must be returned within **10 business days** of the last day of employment using the prepaid
shipping label provided by IT. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from final pay where local law
permits.

## 6. Workspace Standards

The employee is responsible for maintaining a workspace that is safe and fit for purpose. This
means a stable work surface, adequate lighting, a chair suitable for extended seated work, and a
space where confidential conversations can be held without being overheard by people outside the
Company.

Employees must complete the **Remote Workspace Self-Assessment** in the learning portal within 30
days of starting a hybrid or fully remote arrangement, and annually thereafter. The assessment
takes approximately 15 minutes. Failure to complete it does not affect pay but will be flagged to
the manager after two reminders.

Where an employee's self-assessment identifies an ergonomic risk, People Operations will fund a
remote ergonomic assessment and any recommended equipment **outside** the stipend limits in Section
5. Ergonomic accommodations are never charged against the stipend.

### 6.1 Coworking spaces

Employees who cannot work effectively from home — because of space, noise, connectivity, or caring
arrangements — may claim a coworking allowance of up to **$300 per month** on production of an
invoice. The allowance requires manager approval once, not monthly, and continues until the
employee or manager ends it.

Coworking desks must be in a location where the employee can meet the confidentiality requirement
in Section 7. Open coworking floors are acceptable for general work; a private room or booth is
required for anything involving Restricted data.

Employees who use a coworking space for more than 15 days in a month are not additionally eligible
for the internet allowance in Section 5.3 for that month.

### 6.2 Remote onboarding

New employees joining a hybrid or fully remote role receive equipment at their home address at
least **three working days** before their start date. IT confirms delivery; a start date is moved
if equipment has not arrived.

Every remote joiner is assigned an onboarding partner from outside their immediate team for their
first **90 days**, and has a standing weekly 30-minute check-in with their manager for the first
**8 weeks**. Managers are accountable for ensuring a remote joiner has met every member of their
immediate team one-to-one within the first month.

## 7. Information Security Requirements

Location does not change security obligations. Employees working outside a Company office must:

- Use the Company VPN for any access to internal systems not published through the identity portal.
- Keep full-disk encryption enabled. This is enforced by the device management agent; disabling it
  will lock the device out of Company systems.
- Lock the screen whenever the device is unattended, including at home.
- Never use public or open Wi-Fi networks without the VPN active.
- Never print documents classified Confidential or Restricted outside a Company office.
- Report a lost or stolen device to the Security team within **two hours** of discovery, via the
  24-hour security hotline, not by email.

Working from a shared or public space (café, coworking desk, airport lounge) is permitted for
general work, but is **not permitted** for work involving Restricted data, customer personal data,
or unreleased financial information. A privacy screen is required for any work in a public space
and may be claimed under the setup stipend.

## 8. Working From a Temporary Location

An employee may work from a location other than their registered work location for up to **20
working days per calendar year** without changing their arrangement, provided the temporary
location is in a country where Aster Dynamics already has a registered entity, and provided core
hours in Section 4 can still be met.

Temporary work from another location must be logged in Workday **before** travel using the
"Temporary Remote Work" request type. Approval is by the direct manager. The 20-day allowance is
per calendar year and does not carry over.

Days spent working from a temporary location while on Company business travel do not count toward
the 20-day allowance.

## 9. Relocation and International Remote Work

Any permanent change of country requires approval from People Operations, the employee's director,
and Tax **before** the move is committed. Employees must submit the request at least **60 days**
before the intended move date.

Aster Dynamics can only employ people in countries where it holds a registered entity. As of the
effective date of this policy, that list is: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom,
Ireland, Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, India, Singapore, and Australia. A move
to any other country cannot be approved as continued employment.

Relocation may change salary. Aster Dynamics pays against the market of the registered work
location. A move between compensation zones will adjust base salary to the new zone at the next
payroll cycle following the move, up or down. Equity grants already vested are unaffected;
unvested equity continues on its original schedule.

Working from another country, even briefly, can create tax and permanent-establishment exposure for
both the employee and the Company. Employees must not represent themselves as working from a
country other than their registered work location, and must not sign contracts or hold out to
customers as operating from an unregistered location.

## 10. Team Gatherings

Each team holds a minimum of **two in-person gatherings per calendar year**, each of two to four
days. Attendance is expected for all employees including fully remote employees. Travel and
accommodation are funded centrally from the People Operations gathering budget at a standard of
**$1,200 per employee per gathering** for domestic travel and **$2,400** for international travel.

An employee who cannot attend for reasons of health, caring responsibility, or approved leave must
notify their manager as early as practical. Non-attendance does not require a stated reason beyond
one of those categories.

## 11. Changing an Arrangement

Requests to change arrangement are submitted in Workday and reviewed on a **quarterly cycle**, with
decisions communicated within 15 business days of the quarter's review date. Changes take effect at
the start of the following month.

The Company may require a change of arrangement where a role's requirements change materially, with
a minimum of **60 days' notice**. Where such a change requires an employee to begin attending an
office and the employee lives more than 80 kilometres from that office, the Company will either
fund relocation under the standard relocation package or agree an exception.

## 12. Non-Compliance

Persistent failure to meet the attendance minimum in Section 2.2, availability expectations in
Section 4, or security requirements in Section 7 is a performance and, in the case of security, a
conduct matter. It is handled under the Performance Management Policy and the Code of Conduct
respectively, not under this policy.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions to any provision of this policy require written approval from the VP of People
Operations. Requests are submitted through the People Operations service desk. Exceptions are
granted for a defined period, recorded in Workday, and reviewed annually.

## 14. Questions

Questions about this policy go to the People Operations service desk. Questions about equipment go
to the IT service desk. Questions about tax consequences of a move go to `tax@asterdynamics.example`
— managers and People Operations Business Partners are not authorised to give tax advice.
